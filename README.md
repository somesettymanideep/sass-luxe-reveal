# SASS Luxe Portal

Project Overview

Design a world-class premium luxury salon homepage for SASS Hair & Beauty, a leading luxury salon chain operating across Vijayawada, Guntur, and Rajahmundry.

The website should feel similar to international luxury brands such as Toni & Guy, Aveda, Schwarzkopf Professional, and Jean Louis David, while maintaining an elegant Indian premium salon aesthetic.

Overall Design Inspiration

Create a visually stunning, premium salon experience inspired by international luxury brands such as Toni & Guy, Aveda, and Schwarzkopf Professional. The homepage should combine bold black backgrounds, elegant gold accents (#E7CF39), immersive full-width imagery, refined typography, glassmorphism elements, smooth reveal animations, premium hover interactions, and cinematic scrolling effects. The final design should feel sophisticated, luxurious, and conversion-focused, encouraging visitors to book appointments while showcasing SASS Hair & Beauty as the leading luxury salon brand in Andhra Pradesh.
